Company & Timing

An employee's day is marked Present if they work at least the Target Work Hours before punching out, and Late otherwise — regardless of what time they punched in.

Geofencing (Punch In/Out Distance Limit)

When enabled, employees must be within the configured distance of the office to punch in or punch out. Managers and admins are exempt and can punch in/out from anywhere (they're often out visiting sites or clients).

Weekly Off by Department

Choose which weekday(s) are the default off day(s) for each department. New departments fall back to "Default".
